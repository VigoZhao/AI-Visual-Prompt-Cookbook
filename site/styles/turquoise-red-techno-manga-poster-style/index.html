Turquoise Red Techno Manga Poster Style

Retro techno-manga posters with turquoise hardware and red lettering.

Open in gallery View on GitHub

What this style does

A retro techno-manga poster system with a cream paper ground, huge red display lettering, turquoise technical clothing or hardware, dense mechanical linework, annotation panels, cel-shaded figure drawing, and slightly faded printed texture.

Signature traits

  • Preserve the core visual identity: A retro techno-manga poster system with a cream paper ground, huge red display lettering, turquoise technical clothing or hardware, dense mechanical linework, annotation panels, cel-shaded figure drawing, and slightly faded printed texture.
  • Place a huge saturated red-orange custom display headline across the upper third or upper half, behind and around the subject.
  • The headline may use kana-inspired geometric rhythm or condensed block lettering, but it must use new text only.
  • Make turquoise or teal the dominant clothing, casing, or equipment color; balance it with warm gray machinery, black ink, and red-orange accent parts.
  • Keep the perspective mostly flat and poster-like, with compact technical drawing depth only inside the prop or apparatus.
  • Finish as a slightly faded printed manga poster with grain, subtle halftone, mild scan softness, roughened ink edges, and small off-register imperfections.

How to use

  1. Copy the style.json below.
  2. Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude, or any LLM image workflow (Nano Banana Pro, GPT Image, Midjourney).
  3. Swap the variables for your own subject and generate.

Variables

  • SUBJECT subject
  • SUBJECT_ACTION subject action
  • PRODUCT_OR_PROP product or prop
  • LOCATION location
  • BACKGROUND_ELEMENTS background elements
  • MAIN_TEXT main text
  • SECONDARY_TEXT secondary text
  • ACCENT_SYMBOL accent symbol
  • WARDROBE_STYLE wardrobe style
  • ASPECT_RATIO aspect ratio
