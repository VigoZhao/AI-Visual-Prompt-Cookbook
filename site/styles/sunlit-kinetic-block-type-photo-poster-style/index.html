What this style does

A high-energy editorial sports and lifestyle poster style built from full-bleed sunlit photography, oversized cream condensed block typography, diagonal subject crops, compact microcopy clusters, and vivid blue-sky color fields.

Signature traits

  • Full-bleed bright outdoor photographic base with clear blue sky or blue-toned open space occupying a major part of the frame.
  • Immense cream or warm-white condensed block sans typography, cropped by the canvas and partially hidden behind the subject.
  • A single oversized action subject crosses the poster diagonally and breaks through the type layer.
  • Low or close camera angle gives the body, object, or prop exaggerated scale and forward momentum.
  • Bottom and side edges carry secondary text blocks, large numerals, or stacked words in the same cream-white palette.
  • Compact white microcopy appears in short editorial paragraphs or bilingual-style clusters, aligned to strong grid positions.

How to use

  1. Copy the style.json below.
  2. Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude, or any LLM image workflow (Nano Banana Pro, GPT Image, Midjourney).
  3. Swap the variables for your own subject and generate.

Variables

  • SUBJECT subject
  • SUBJECT_ACTION subject action
  • PRODUCT_OR_PROP product or prop
  • LOCATION location
  • BACKGROUND_ELEMENTS background elements
  • MAIN_TEXT main text
  • SECONDARY_TEXT secondary text
  • ACCENT_SYMBOL accent symbol
  • WARDROBE_STYLE wardrobe style
  • STYLE_FIDELITY_ANCHORS style fidelity anchors

  • ASPECT_RATIO aspect ratio
