Chromatic Impact Athlete

High-contrast athletic posters colliding grayscale body photography, extreme perspective, and a single red-orange energy accent.

What this style does

High-contrast athletic editorial posters that turn a frozen movement into a collision of grayscale body photography, extreme perspective, and a single red-orange energy accent.

Signature traits

  • High-contrast monochrome athletic photography or photoreal cutout with deep charcoal blacks, pale gray whites and crisp anatomical form.
  • A single vivid vermilion-to-orange accent is reserved for the featured object, impact zone or directional energy trail.
  • Extreme wide-angle or close-perspective cropping makes one body part or sport object dominate the frame and creates visceral depth.
  • Clean near-white negative space isolates the athlete and lets the action silhouette read immediately at poster scale.
  • Motion is expressed by controlled warm glow, directional blur or heat haze attached to the action, never by random decorative clutter.
  • Typography is compact, bold, condensed and italic, usually in red, with short legible copy integrated into the empty space.

How to use

  1. Copy the style.json below.
  2. Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude, or any LLM image workflow (Nano Banana Pro, GPT Image, Midjourney).
  3. Swap the variables for your own subject and generate.

Variables

  • ASPECT_RATIO aspect ratio
  • STYLE_FIDELITY_ANCHORS style fidelity anchors
  • SOURCE_CONTENT_TO_AVOID source content to avoid
  • SUBJECT subject
  • SUBJECT_ACTION subject action
  • WARDROBE_STYLE wardrobe style
  • PRODUCT_OR_PROP product or prop
  • MAIN_TEXT main text
  • SECONDARY_TEXT secondary text
  • DESIGN_DIRECTION design direction
  • LOCATION location
  • BACKGROUND_ELEMENTS background elements
  • ACCENT_SYMBOL accent symbol
