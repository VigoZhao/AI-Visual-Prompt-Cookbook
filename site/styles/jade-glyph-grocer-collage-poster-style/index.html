Jade Glyph Grocer Collage Poster Style

Cream grocer posters with jade glyphs, vegetable silhouettes, and glossy produce-photo centerpieces.

What this style does

A sparse East Asian grocer-poster system built from warm cream paper, oversized jade-green hand-cut glyphs, pale vegetable silhouette clouds, tiny editorial headers, and one glossy produce-photo centerpiece layered over the typography.

Signature traits

  • Warm cream or ivory poster paper dominates the canvas, with no scenic background and only soft paper grain.
  • One saturated jade or produce-green ink family controls the headline, accent glyphs, and most graphic marks.
  • Oversized hand-cut glyphs are rough, blocky, irregular, and arranged like abstract CJK poster lettering rather than clean typography.
  • A realistic glossy product photo is layered in front of the glyphs, cropped large enough to become the main dimensional object.
  • Pale mint botanical silhouette clouds sit behind the type and product as soft flat shape fields, not detailed illustrations.
  • Tiny black editorial labels sit along the top edge with small square bullets and spaced uppercase micro text.

How to use

  1. Copy the style.json below.
  2. Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude, or any LLM image workflow (Nano Banana Pro, GPT Image, Midjourney).
  3. Swap the variables for your own subject and generate.

Variables

  • SUBJECT subject
  • SUBJECT_ACTION subject action
  • PRODUCT_OR_PROP product or prop
  • LOCATION location
  • BACKGROUND_ELEMENTS background elements
  • MAIN_TEXT main text
  • SECONDARY_TEXT secondary text
  • ACCENT_SYMBOL accent symbol
  • WARDROBE_STYLE wardrobe style
  • STYLE_FIDELITY_ANCHORS style fidelity anchors
  • SOURCE_CONTENT_TO_AVOID source content to avoid
  • ASPECT_RATIO aspect ratio
