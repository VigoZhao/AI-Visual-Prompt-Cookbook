What this style does

A high-density blue creator-tech advertisement style built around macro 3D hardware heroes, oversized ribbed gloves, electric-blue HUD panels, massive condensed typography, glossy glass cards, and one warm gold performance badge.

Signature traits

  • Macro product-hero composition with the object large, tilted, and centered or slightly off-center, filling the frame.
  • Oversized ribbed gloves or flexible striped fingers crop in from the frame edges and physically frame the product.
  • Deep glossy 3D rendering with rounded plastic, rubber, metal, and glass surfaces, not a flat poster or hand-drawn illustration.
  • Cold electric-blue background with dark navy gradients, diagonal light streaks, faint grid lines, and misty volumetric glow.
  • Large condensed block headline typography in white or icy gray, partially hidden behind the product or cropped by the frame.
  • Layered translucent glass HUD cards with rounded rectangles, radial meters, small icons, and compact spec labels.

How to use

  1. Copy the style.json below.
  2. Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude, or any LLM image workflow (Nano Banana Pro, GPT Image, Midjourney).
  3. Swap the variables for your own subject and generate.

Variables

  • SUBJECT subject
  • SUBJECT_ACTION subject action
  • PRODUCT_OR_PROP product or prop
