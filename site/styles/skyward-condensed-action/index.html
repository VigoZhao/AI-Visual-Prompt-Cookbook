Skyward Condensed Action

Worm-eye airborne sports photography against open cool blue sky, with monumental ultra-condensed fluorescent type occluded by a foreshortened silhouette.

What this style does

Worm-eye airborne sports photography against expansive cool blue sky, with monumental ultra-condensed fluorescent uppercase type occluded by a sharply foreshortened human silhouette.

Signature traits

  • Monumental EXTRA-BOLD ULTRA-CONDENSED upright uppercase sans lettering forms a dense graphic wall behind the photographic figure; very tall narrow glyphs with narrow counters, not wide geometric or lean italic lettering.
  • Dramatic camera from below captures a believable airborne adult and near/far foreshortening against an expansive cool cyan-to-petrol blue sky with fine white clouds; one connected figure, natural body proportions under perspective.
  • One flat electric lime-green ink makes the headline and tiny supporting text; skin, pale neutral and deep navy clothing remain photographic. The figure overlaps part of the headline while its full wording stays inferable.
  • Airborne gesture and object relationships follow the activity: reaching, folding, rotating or clearing an obstacle. Do not reuse the source high kick.
  • Arrange the towering condensed headline around the action and integrate it through readable silhouette overlap. Choose line breaks and placement by subject shape; avoid a detached text column or two text walls squeezing the figure.
  • Vary camera distance, roll, cloud concentration and subject scale so the airborne action organizes the canvas. Sky forms useful breathing spaces around movement, not an accidental empty lower band. Do not fill space with decoration or oversized type alone.

How to use

  1. Copy the style.json below.
  2. Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude, or any LLM image workflow (Nano Banana Pro, GPT Image, Midjourney).
  3. Swap the variables for your own subject and generate.

Variables

  • ASPECT_RATIO aspect ratio
  • STYLE_FIDELITY_ANCHORS style fidelity anchors
  • SOURCE_CONTENT_TO_AVOID source content to avoid
  • SUBJECT subject
  • MAIN_TEXT main text
  • SMALL_TEXT small text
  • ACCENT accent
  • DIRECTION direction
  • WIDE_DIRECTION wide direction
  • SUBJECT_ACTION subject action
  • PRODUCT_OR_PROP product or prop
  • LOCATION location
  • BACKGROUND_ELEMENTS background elements
  • SECONDARY_TEXT secondary text
  • ACCENT_SYMBOL accent symbol
  • WARDROBE_STYLE wardrobe style
