Slice Signal Editorial

Experimental editorial posters with oversized condensed headlines, scarlet blocks, and photograph subjects cut by vertical slices.

What this style does

Restrained, sharp experimental editorial posters built from oversized condensed headlines, black-white-and-scarlet blocks, and photographic subjects interrupted by vertical slices.

Signature traits

  • Oversized uppercase condensed grotesk display type is a primary structural element, with forceful narrow proportions and strong contrast against quieter fields; keep at least one complete phrase readable.
  • Treat a realistic photographic person, object, or scene with narrow vertical shutter-like slices that selectively shift, stretch, repeat, or interrupt image bands while leaving the subject recognizable.
  • Build a stark palette from near-black, cool off-white, and one decisive signal-red accent; vary their proportions while keeping the overall contrast sharp.
  • Use hard rectangular fields, abrupt horizontal cuts, and fine divider lines to organize the image like a tightly edited print poster.
  • Pair the giant display title with a restrained secondary line in small clean sans-serif or slim italic lettering; it remains subordinate and intentional.
  • Keep type fields crisp and graphic while any scan-like grain or softness stays subtle and localized to the photographic image.

How to use

  1. Copy the style.json below.
  2. Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude, or any LLM image workflow (Nano Banana Pro, GPT Image, Midjourney).
  3. Swap the variables for your own subject and generate.

Variables

  • ASPECT_RATIO aspect ratio
  • STYLE_FIDELITY_ANCHORS style fidelity anchors
  • SOURCE_CONTENT_TO_AVOID source content to avoid
  • SUBJECT subject
  • ACTION action
  • MAIN_TEXT main text
  • SECONDARY_TEXT secondary text
  • DESIGN_DIRECTION design direction
  • SUBJECT_ACTION subject action
  • PRODUCT_OR_PROP product or prop
  • LOCATION location
  • BACKGROUND_ELEMENTS background elements
  • ACCENT_SYMBOL accent symbol
  • WARDROBE_STYLE wardrobe style
