Gothic Cat Doodle Photo Collage Style

Dramatic architecture photos with playful cartoon creature overlays.

What this style does

A playful photo-illustration collage style combining dramatic real architectural photography with oversized flat cartoon creature overlays, bubbly hand-drawn headline lettering, and loose marker doodles.

Signature traits

  • Preserve the core visual identity: A playful photo-illustration collage style combining dramatic real architectural photography with oversized flat cartoon creature overlays, bubbly hand-drawn headline lettering, and loose marker doodles.
  • Use BACKGROUND_ELEMENTS to control secondary scene details, textures, signage, props, and environmental cues.
  • Use a realistic low-angle architectural photograph as the base layer.
  • Keep the facade detailed, recognizable, and photographic, with dramatic vertical perspective and a saturated teal sky.
  • Overlay one oversized flat cartoon creature in saturated tomato red or another bold color, with rough black marker contours, simple expressive features, cropped limbs, and small yellow edge highlights.
  • Add small doodle circles, swooshes, sparks, motion curves, and tiny secondary cartoon figures in windows or doorways for scale contrast.

How to use

  1. Copy the style.json below.
  2. Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude, or any LLM image workflow (Nano Banana Pro, GPT Image, Midjourney).
  3. Swap the variables for your own subject and generate.

Variables

  • SUBJECT subject
  • SUBJECT_ACTION subject action
  • PRODUCT_OR_PROP product or prop
  • LOCATION location
  • BACKGROUND_ELEMENTS background elements
  • MAIN_TEXT main text
  • SECONDARY_TEXT secondary text
  • ACCENT_SYMBOL accent symbol
  • WARDROBE_STYLE wardrobe style
  • ASPECT_RATIO aspect ratio
