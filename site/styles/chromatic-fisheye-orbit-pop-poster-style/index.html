Chromatic Fisheye Orbit Pop Poster Style

Pop fisheye posters with orbiting type and chromatic arcs.

Open in gallery View on GitHub

What this style does

A high-key pop poster system built from extreme fisheye photography, a convex glass-dome center, oversized orbiting typography, hot red-magenta-orange letter fills, aqua chromatic light arcs, and light analog print texture.

Signature traits

  • Preserve the core visual identity: A high-key pop poster system built from extreme fisheye photography, a convex glass-dome center, oversized orbiting typography, hot red-magenta-orange letter fills, aqua chromatic light arcs, and light analog print texture.
  • Photography: build a finished high-key pop culture poster, not a sketch.
  • Use an extreme fisheye or convex glass-dome viewpoint from below, with the subject centered inside a circular lens bubble.
  • Keep people, props, hair, fabric, and hands photographic and realistic while allowing wide-angle edge distortion.
  • Layout: keep the surrounding field mostly warm off-white and pale grey so the graphic system has open space.
  • Hold the subject in a central circular dome and let the composition feel off-axis, kinetic, and orbiting without cluttering the center.

How to use

  1. Copy the style.json below.
  2. Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude, or any LLM image workflow (Nano Banana Pro, GPT Image, Midjourney).
  3. Swap the variables for your own subject and generate.

Variables

  • SUBJECT subject
  • SUBJECT_ACTION subject action
  • PRODUCT_OR_PROP product or prop
  • LOCATION location
  • BACKGROUND_ELEMENTS background elements
  • MAIN_TEXT main text
  • SECONDARY_TEXT secondary text
  • ACCENT_SYMBOL accent symbol
  • WARDROBE_STYLE wardrobe style
  • ASPECT_RATIO aspect ratio
