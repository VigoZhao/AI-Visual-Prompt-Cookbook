Rough Ink Music Doodle Poster Style

Hand-inked music posters with brush lettering and playful doodles.

Open in gallery View on GitHub

What this style does

A rough hand-inked poster style built from oversized dark green-black brush lettering, pale blush paper, hot pink secondary type, naive mascot drawings, teal and pink flat fills, sharp yellow burst marks, scattered music-note doodles, and scanned risograph-like print texture.

Signature traits

  • Pale blush paper background with a lightly scanned, warm off-white finish.
  • Oversized rough brush headline in deep green-black ink, with broken edges and uneven pressure.
  • Hot pink secondary typography placed as small stacked text blocks, labels, or date-like graphic notes.
  • Flat naive mascot or figure drawings with thick scratchy outlines and simplified anatomy.
  • Limited accent palette: deep green-black linework, hot pink fills, teal green fills, and acidic yellow bursts.
  • Poster layout uses asymmetrical open space with separate floating illustration clusters rather than a boxed grid.

How to use

  1. Copy the style.json below.
  2. Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude, or any LLM image workflow (Nano Banana Pro, GPT Image, Midjourney).
  3. Swap the variables for your own subject and generate.

  • SUBJECT subject
  • SUBJECT_ACTION subject action
