Sky Blue Home Life Doodle Poster Style

Sky-blue home-life posters with house insets, giant black lettering, badges, and marker doodle scenes.

Open in gallery View on GitHub

What this style does

A naive flat poster system with a full sky-blue field, a rough house-shaped white inset, giant uneven black hand-lettering, small badge labels, and simple thick-outlined cartoon home-life scenes drawn like marker doodles.

Signature traits

  • Full-bleed pastel sky-blue background acts as the outer poster field.
  • A large off-white house-shaped inset with a rough black outline contains the scene.
  • Oversized black hand-lettered headline sits at the top with uneven stroke width and playful imbalance.
  • A small red rounded capsule badge and slanted secondary text add event-poster energy.
  • Characters and props use thick wobbly black marker outlines with flat color fills and almost no shading.
  • Cartoon anatomy is intentionally chunky, with large rounded pink limbs, simple dot eyes, and exaggerated hands or feet.

How to use

  1. Copy the style.json below.
  2. Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude, or any LLM image workflow (Nano Banana Pro, GPT Image, Midjourney).
  3. Swap the variables for your own subject and generate.

Variables

  • SUBJECT subject
  • SUBJECT_ACTION subject action
  • PRODUCT_OR_PROP product or prop
