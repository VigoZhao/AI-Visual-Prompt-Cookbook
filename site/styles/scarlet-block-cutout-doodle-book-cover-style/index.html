Scarlet Block Cutout Doodle Book Cover Style

Literary white-paper covers with scarlet letterforms, central cutout objects, marker contours, and asymmetrical space.

Open in gallery View on GitHub

What this style does

A stark literary cover system built from a white paper field, oversized scarlet serif letterforms, one central photoreal cutout object, rough black marker contour drawing, small bookish serif typography, and strong asymmetrical negative space.

Signature traits

  • Pure white or warm-white paper field with generous margins and no scenic background.
  • Two to four enormous scarlet red serif or slab letterforms cropped by the frame and stacked behind the subject.
  • One central photoreal cutout subject placed in front of the red letters, with visible object detail and a subtle drop shadow.
  • A rough thick black marker contour drawing overlays and partly surrounds the subject, with uneven hand-drawn edges.
  • The marker line forms a loose silhouette, loop, or invented body-like extension rather than a clean vector outline.
  • Small uppercase serif typography appears as book-cover furniture: top-left name line, right-edge vertical title, and tiny footer.

How to use

  1. Copy the style.json below.
  2. Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude, or any LLM image workflow (Nano Banana Pro, GPT Image, Midjourney).
