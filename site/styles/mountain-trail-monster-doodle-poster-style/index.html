Mountain Trail Monster Doodle Poster Style

Outdoor hiking photos remixed with monster companions and annotations.

Open in gallery View on GitHub

What this style does

A candid outdoor hiking photograph remixed with a flat hand-drawn monster companion, oversized Spanish comic lettering, and loose sketch annotations, creating a playful adventure-poster collage.

Signature traits

  • Preserve the core visual identity: A candid outdoor hiking photograph remixed with a flat hand-drawn monster companion, oversized Spanish comic lettering, and loose sketch annotations, creating a playful adventure-poster collage.
  • Use BACKGROUND_ELEMENTS to control secondary scene details, textures, signage, props, and environmental cues.
  • Use a realistic candid outdoor hiking photograph as the base layer, with natural daylight, real mountain or trail detail, trees, sky, rocks, and a believable person in practical outdoor clothing.
  • Keep the person photographic and naturally integrated into the landscape.
  • Use off-white letter fill, black outline, yellow/red/cobalt offset shadows, light inner scribbles, uneven scale, and a playful Spanish travel-poster feeling.
  • Avoid watermark, username, creator ID, platform logo, QR code, app UI, realistic demon, horror, blood, violence, 3D mascot, plush toy, full cartoon background, corporate layout, minimalist typography, dense text, studio portrait, and low-detail mountains.

How to use

  1. Copy the style.json below.
  2. Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude, or any LLM image workflow (Nano Banana Pro, GPT Image, Midjourney).
  3. Swap the variables for your own subject and generate.

Variables

  • SUBJECT subject
  • SUBJECT_ACTION subject action
  • PRODUCT_OR_PROP product or prop
  • LOCATION location
  • BACKGROUND_ELEMENTS background elements
  • MAIN_TEXT main text
  • SECONDARY_TEXT secondary text
  • ACCENT_SYMBOL accent symbol
  • WARDROBE_STYLE wardrobe style
  • ASPECT_RATIO aspect ratio
