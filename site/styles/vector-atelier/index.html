Visual OS 051 — Freeze the Gear

Full-bleed future-sport posters isolating one razor-sharp piece of gear against directional motion trails and a giant white slogan.

What this style does

Full-bleed future-sport posters isolate one razor-sharp piece of gear against directional motion trails, luminous gradients and a single giant white geometric slogan.

Signature traits

  • Make one sport object razor-sharp and freeze it as the only stable visual anchor; all supporting action, people, surfaces and lights streak with directional motion blur.
  • Use a luminous saturated gradient field with a clear directional light sweep, rich color separation and a softly photographic sports-campaign finish.
  • Set one giant white geometric sans-serif slogan in uppercase, cropped or angled through the frame as an active compositional shape; keep all other copy absent or microscopic.
  • Compose full-bleed edge-to-edge posters with an oversized foreground object, aggressive perspective and a readable path of motion from one side of the frame to the other.
  • Preserve tactile material realism on the frozen anchor: rubber, leather, textile, metal, wood or composite surfaces must remain crisp while the surrounding world smears.
  • Let each case use its own saturated color atmosphere and sport context while preserving the one-anchor, giant-type, motion-trail grammar.

How to use

  1. Copy the style.json below.
  2. Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude, or any LLM image workflow (Nano Banana Pro, GPT Image, Midjourney).
  3. Swap the variables for your own subject and generate.

  • STYLE_FIDELITY_ANCHORS style fidelity anchors
  • SOURCE_CONTENT_TO_AVOID source content to avoid

  • GRADIENT gradient

  • PRODUCT_OR_PROP product or prop
