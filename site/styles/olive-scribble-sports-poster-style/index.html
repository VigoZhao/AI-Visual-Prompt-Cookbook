Olive Scribble Sports Poster Style

Handmade sports posters with olive blocks and kinetic scribbles.

What this style does

A kinetic flat editorial illustration style built from off-white paper, large olive-green poster blocks, oversized action figures, rough black ink contours, red marker motion arcs, yellow-green dry-brush swaths, sparse debris, and handmade screenprint texture.

Signature traits

  • Off-white paper ground with a large irregular olive or yellow-green block covering most of the background.
  • One oversized action subject placed diagonally across the composition.
  • Flat screenprint color areas with minimal shading and no smooth gradients.
  • Heavy black ink silhouettes, angular brush contours, and chaotic scratch lines.
  • Red marker arcs, arrows, and motion loops crossing the subject.
  • Yellow-green dry-brush swaths or spiral strokes behind the action.

How to use

  1. Copy the style.json below.
  2. Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude, or any LLM image workflow (Nano Banana Pro, GPT Image, Midjourney).
  3. Swap the variables for your own subject and generate.

Variables

  • SUBJECT subject
  • SUBJECT_ACTION subject action
  • PRODUCT_OR_PROP product or prop
  • LOCATION location
  • BACKGROUND_ELEMENTS background elements
  • MAIN_TEXT main text
  • SECONDARY_TEXT secondary text
  • ACCENT_SYMBOL accent symbol
  • WARDROBE_STYLE wardrobe style
  • STYLE_FIDELITY_ANCHORS style fidelity anchors
  • SOURCE_CONTENT_TO_AVOID source content to avoid
  • ASPECT_RATIO aspect ratio
  • NEGATIVE_PROMPT negative prompt
