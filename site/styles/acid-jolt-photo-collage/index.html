Acid Jolt Portrait Collage

Punchy portrait posters mixing warm low-fi close-ups, blunt photo fragments, massive lemon-yellow lettering, and loose marker signals.

Open in gallery View on GitHub

What this style does

A punchy portrait-poster system mixing warm, low-fi close-up photography, blunt photo-fragment inserts, massive lemon-yellow rounded lettering, and loose marker signals against dark, quiet fields.

Signature traits

  • Build the image around a large, close, warm-toned photographic portrait or human detail, with realistic peach, coral, and brown skin against a much darker, muted field.
  • Use a small number of deliberately blunt rectangular or trapezoid photo fragments, visibly layered across or beside the main photo; the inserts may isolate a face detail, hand, clothing, or case-relevant prop without repeating one fixed anatomy layout.
  • Make one short all-caps headline visually dominant in thick, rounded, tightly spaced sans-serif letters, usually lemon or acid yellow; let the type press into the photo layers while keeping the complete wording readable.
  • Hold a stark warm-light / dark-field / acid-yellow contrast, with yellow reserved for the headline and a few hand-drawn graphic accents.
  • Add one or two loose, hand-marked yellow strokes or simple signals that interact with the subject's gesture or theme; keep them irregular and editorial, not a dense technical interface.
  • Preserve an intentionally low-fi photographic finish: visible grain or compression texture, slight softness, uneven flash-lit tonal transitions, and crisp collage boundaries where fragments overlap.

How to use

  1. Copy the style.json below.
  2. Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude, or any LLM image workflow (Nano Banana Pro, GPT Image, Midjourney).
  3. Swap the variables for your own subject and generate.

  • ASPECT_RATIO aspect ratio
  • STYLE_FIDELITY_ANCHORS style fidelity anchors
  • SOURCE_CONTENT_TO_AVOID source content to avoid
  • SUBJECT subject
  • SUBJECT_ACTION subject action

  • MAIN_TEXT main text
  • SECONDARY_TEXT secondary text
  • DESIGN_DIRECTION design direction
  • LOCATION location
  • BACKGROUND_ELEMENTS background elements
  • ACCENT_SYMBOL accent symbol
