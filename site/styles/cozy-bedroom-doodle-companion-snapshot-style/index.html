What this style does

A candid low-light home photo style with a large flat 2D cushion-doll companion composited into the lower foreground, creating a quiet late-night creative diary mood with warm room texture, screen glow, and tiny handwritten doodle marks.

Signature traits

  • Preserve the core visual identity: A candid low-light home photo style with a large flat 2D cushion-doll companion composited into the lower foreground, creating a quiet late-night creative diary mood with warm room texture, screen glow, and tiny handwritten doodle marks.
  • Use BACKGROUND_ELEMENTS to control secondary scene details, textures, signage, props, and environmental cues.
  • Use DOODLE_COMPANION as a hand-drawn companion layered into the real room snapshot.
  • Use DOODLE_SCALE to set the companion scale relative to the subject and room objects.
  • Use LIGHT_SOURCE to preserve the snapshot lighting direction and warm room mood.
  • Use a realistic candid phone-photo as the base layer.

How to use

  1. Copy the style.json below.
  2. Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude, or any LLM image workflow (Nano Banana Pro, GPT Image, Midjourney).
  3. Swap the variables for your own subject and generate.

Variables

  • SUBJECT subject
  • SUBJECT_ACTION subject action
  • PRODUCT_OR_PROP product or prop
  • LOCATION location
  • BACKGROUND_ELEMENTS background elements
  • MAIN_TEXT main text
  • SECONDARY_TEXT secondary text
  • ACCENT_SYMBOL accent symbol
  • WARDROBE_STYLE wardrobe style
  • DOODLE_COMPANION doodle companion
  • DOODLE_SCALE doodle scale
  • CHARACTER_DIFFERENCE_RULE character difference rule
  • LIGHT_SOURCE light source
  • ASPECT_RATIO aspect ratio
