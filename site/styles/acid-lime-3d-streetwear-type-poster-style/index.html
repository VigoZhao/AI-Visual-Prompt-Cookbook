Acid Lime 3D Streetwear Type Poster Style

Glossy C4D streetwear campaigns with black block type and acid-lime accents.

What this style does

A glossy C4D streetwear campaign poster system built from pale studio space, oversized black block typography, acid-lime marker strokes, sticker badges, fashion-tool props, low wide-angle character staging, and clean synthetic product lighting.

Signature traits

  • Pale cool-gray or off-white studio background with large empty breathing space around a dense central campaign cluster.
  • Glossy C4D character or product rendering with smooth plastic skin, simplified doll-like features, tactile fabric, chrome, and black rubber surfaces.
  • Low wide-angle camera perspective that makes foreground shoes, tools, bags, or props feel oversized and close to the viewer.
  • Heavy black condensed block headline typography acts as a massive graphic object, stacked and slightly compressed rather than quiet caption text.
  • Acid-lime green is the main accent, appearing as fabric fields, marker signatures, object trim, sticker dots, tags, and thin energetic strokes.
  • Small sticker-badge grammar surrounds the scene: warning triangles, smiley dots, barcode marks, capsule labels, scalloped seals, and tiny black tags.

How to use

  1. Copy the style.json below.
  2. Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude, or any LLM image workflow (Nano Banana Pro, GPT Image, Midjourney).
  3. Swap the variables for your own subject and generate.

Variables

  • SUBJECT subject
