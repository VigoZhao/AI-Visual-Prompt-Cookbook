Playful Mascot Doodle Snapshot Style

Real-life snapshots layered with mascot stickers and doodles.

Open in gallery View on GitHub

What this style does

A casual real-life social photo transformed into a playful poster by layering original cartoon mascot stickers, hand-drawn outlines, ribbon headline panels, sparkles, spirals, and sketchy decorative marks over the photographic scene.

Signature traits

  • Preserve the core visual identity: A casual real-life social photo transformed into a playful poster by layering original cartoon mascot stickers, hand-drawn outlines, ribbon headline panels, sparkles, spirals, and sketchy decorative marks over the photographic scene.
  • Use BACKGROUND_ELEMENTS to control secondary scene details, textures, signage, props, and environmental cues.
  • Use a realistic casual phone-photo scene as the base layer.
  • Keep the environment photographic, naturally lit, and location-specific.
  • Add original flat cartoon mascot stickers on top of the photo: fruit, cloud, star, cup, wave, and jelly-shaped characters with simple eyes, thick dark outlines, glossy color blocks, sunny yellow, teal, coral, sky blue, lavender, cream, and soft pink accents.
  • Add a loose warm outline around the main subject, plus hand-drawn sparkles, spirals, web lines, stars, arrows, and marker doodles floating over the scene.

How to use

  1. Copy the style.json below.
  2. Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude, or any LLM image workflow (Nano Banana Pro, GPT Image, Midjourney).
  3. Swap the variables for your own subject and generate.

Variables

  • SUBJECT subject
  • SUBJECT_ACTION subject action
  • PRODUCT_OR_PROP product or prop
  • LOCATION location
  • BACKGROUND_ELEMENTS background elements
  • MAIN_TEXT main text
  • SECONDARY_TEXT secondary text
  • ACCENT_SYMBOL accent symbol
  • WARDROBE_STYLE wardrobe style
  • ASPECT_RATIO aspect ratio
