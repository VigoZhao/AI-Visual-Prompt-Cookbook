Clean Triptych Travel Vlog Thumbnail Style

Clean travel thumbnails with three photo panels and soft notes.

What this style does

A clean travel-vlog thumbnail system built from three vertical photographic panels, oversized lowercase white destination type, small italic travel annotations, sparse hand-drawn marks, and a soft phone-camera editorial finish.

Signature traits

  • Preserve the core visual identity: A clean travel-vlog thumbnail system built from three vertical photographic panels, oversized lowercase white destination type, small italic travel annotations, sparse hand-drawn marks, and a soft phone-camera editorial finish.
  • Use BACKGROUND_ELEMENTS to control secondary scene details, textures, signage, props, and environmental cues.
  • Use a clean three-panel travel-vlog thumbnail composition.
  • Divide the frame into three equal vertical photographic panels with hard edges and no visible border.
  • Place a huge lowercase destination headline in pure white across the center of the whole image, crossing all three panels.
  • Use an extra-bold rounded geometric sans-serif, smooth and friendly, with no outline and minimal shadow.

How to use

  1. Copy the style.json below.
  2. Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude, or any LLM image workflow (Nano Banana Pro, GPT Image, Midjourney).
  3. Swap the variables for your own subject and generate.

Variables

  • SUBJECT subject
  • SUBJECT_ACTION subject action
  • PRODUCT_OR_PROP product or prop
  • LOCATION location
  • BACKGROUND_ELEMENTS background elements
  • MAIN_TEXT main text
  • SECONDARY_TEXT secondary text
  • ACCENT_SYMBOL accent symbol
  • WARDROBE_STYLE wardrobe style
  • ASPECT_RATIO aspect ratio
