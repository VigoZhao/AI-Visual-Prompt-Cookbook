Loose Scribble Riso Print Style

Sparse riso posters with wavering contours, overprint accents, handwritten margins, and visible paper grain.

Open in gallery View on GitHub

What this style does

A sparse handmade riso or screenprint poster style with one large simplified subject, wavering black contour drawing, rough off-white paper, flat blue and coral-red overprint accents, handwritten margin text, and visible print grain.

Signature traits

  • Sparse single-subject composition with one large central object and generous off-white negative space.
  • Handmade rectangular border with wavering black lines close to the page edge.
  • Thick loose black contour strokes mixed with thin wandering scribble lines.
  • Flat riso or screenprint color fields with slight registration offset and uneven ink coverage.
  • Palette dominated by off-white paper, rough black ink, one sky or cornflower blue block, and one coral-red accent.
  • Simplified object silhouette with playful distortion instead of realistic perspective.

How to use

  1. Copy the style.json below.
  2. Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude, or any LLM image workflow (Nano Banana Pro, GPT Image, Midjourney).
  3. Swap the variables for your own subject and generate.

Variables

  • SUBJECT subject
  • SUBJECT_ACTION subject action
  • PRODUCT_OR_PROP product or prop
  • LOCATION location
  • BACKGROUND_ELEMENTS background elements
  • MAIN_TEXT main text
  • SECONDARY_TEXT secondary text
  • ACCENT_SYMBOL accent symbol
  • WARDROBE_STYLE wardrobe style
  • STYLE_FIDELITY_ANCHORS style fidelity anchors
  • SOURCE_CONTENT_TO_AVOID source content to avoid
