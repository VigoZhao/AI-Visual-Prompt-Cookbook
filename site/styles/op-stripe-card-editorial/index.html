Op Stripe Card Editorial

Playing-card editorial language with optical black ink ribbons, monumental high-contrast serif type, and a crisp colored figure on textured light paper.

Open in gallery View on GitHub

What this style does

Playing-card editorial language meets optical black ink ribbons, monumental high-contrast serif type and a crisp colored photographic person on subtly textured light paper.

Signature traits

  • Flowing black ink stripes alternate with light paper, bending, fanning or converging into a strong optical field that participates in the composition; flat graphic ribbons rather than glossy 3D tubes.
  • Monumental high-contrast display serif lettering and a crisp full-color photographic cutout share the main hierarchy against the nearly monochrome graphic field.
  • Playing-card-like rank/suit indexing and small editorial marginalia frame the design, mixing compact sans copy with expressive brush handwriting; typography remains readable.
  • Stripe geometry may express gesture, air, force or structure. Its direction, negative space and relationship to the figure can change by case; a central spiral is not mandatory.
  • Pose, crop, figure placement and headline shape are open design decisions guided by the theme. Choose a different spatial relationship per case without replicating the source seated silhouette.
  • Use a restrained wardrobe or prop color against black and light neutral paper, chosen for each theme; do not lock orange trousers, grey knitwear or a particular suit.

How to use

  1. Copy the style.json below.
  2. Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude, or any LLM image workflow (Nano Banana Pro, GPT Image, Midjourney).
  3. Swap the variables for your own subject and generate.
