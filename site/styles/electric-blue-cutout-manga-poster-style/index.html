Electric Blue Cutout Manga Poster Style

Electric-blue manga posters with white cutout geometry, rounded modular type, orange microtype, and a cel-shaded subject in exaggerated perspective.

Open in gallery View on GitHub

What this style does

A reusable illustrated poster style built from saturated electric-blue fields, sharp white cutout geometry, oversized rounded modular typography, warm orange microtype, and one cel-shaded manga subject with a large foreground prop in exaggerated perspective.

Signature traits

  • Dominant saturated electric-blue field with hard white cutout geometry at the edges.
  • Huge rounded modular white display letterforms cropped beyond the frame and used as poster architecture.
  • Warm orange-yellow supporting type in compact labels, tilted subheads, and small information blocks.
  • One cel-shaded manga or anime subject with clean dark keylines and simplified blocked shadows.
  • Large foreground prop or product in exaggerated three-quarter perspective, overlapping the lower edge.
  • Sparse graphic-stage composition with only a few background cues rather than a detailed environment.

How to use

  1. Copy the style.json below.
  2. Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude, or any LLM image workflow (Nano Banana Pro, GPT Image, Midjourney).
  3. Swap the variables for your own subject and generate.

Variables

  • SUBJECT subject
  • SUBJECT_ACTION subject action
