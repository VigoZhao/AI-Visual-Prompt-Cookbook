Grainy Envelope Flyer Collage

Tactile event posters with repeated photocopied flyers spilling from a saturated paper envelope, framed by cutout icons and spare black type.

Open in gallery View on GitHub

What this style does

A tactile event poster built around repeated photocopied flyers emerging from a saturated paper envelope, framed by cutout icons and spare black type.

Signature traits

  • An open colored paper envelope holds three overlapping cool white event flyers, fanning upward at different small angles; visible triangular envelope folds anchor the lower collage.
  • Each flyer repeats the same bold black condensed headline and rough monochrome line illustration; at least one headline is fully visible.
  • A compact irregular cut-paper masthead and small factual text occupy open background outside the central stack.
  • Several theme-specific cutout stickers orbit the stack with selective white borders, varied scale and shallow overlaps.
  • Flat printed collage with fine paper grain and rough ink lines, not a dimensional product photograph.
  • Background and envelope colors may change by theme but retain strong chromatic contrast against cool white sheets and black ink.

How to use

  1. Copy the style.json below.
  2. Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude, or any LLM image workflow (Nano Banana Pro, GPT Image, Midjourney).
  3. Swap the variables for your own subject and generate.

  • STYLE_FIDELITY_ANCHORS style fidelity anchors
  • SOURCE_CONTENT_TO_AVOID source content to avoid
