Neon Outdoor Diary Longform Collage Style

Longform outdoor diary collages with acid-green type and torn field-note panels.

★ Star on GitHub Open in gallery View on GitHub

4 example cases

Same style.json, different subjects. Each case's values are in the examples section of the JSON.

What this style does

A tall mobile-first outdoor diary poster system with a charcoal black canvas, documentary action-photo cutouts, acid green kinetic headlines, ripped paper panels, hand-drawn arrows, sticker labels, numbered sections, and dense social-note pacing.

Signature traits

  • Charcoal black background with thin vertical mobile-scroll framing and high-density stacked sections.
  • Documentary outdoor photo-collage subject cutouts with rough white keyline edges and mixed scale.
  • Acid green and white oversized condensed headline typography on slanted black label strips.
  • Orange hand-drawn arrows, loops, lightning marks, route lines, and small doodle bursts around content blocks.
  • Ripped notebook paper or torn tape panels used as schedule, rules, or checklist sections.
  • Numbered chapter badges that feel like a personal field diary rather than a clean corporate infographic.

How to use

  1. Copy the style.json below.
  2. Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude, or any LLM image workflow (Nano Banana Pro, GPT Image, Midjourney).
  3. Swap the variables for your own subject and generate.

Variables

  • SUBJECT subject
  • SUBJECT_ACTION subject action
  • PRODUCT_OR_PROP product or prop
  • LOCATION location
  • BACKGROUND_ELEMENTS background elements
  • MAIN_TEXT main text
  • SECONDARY_TEXT secondary text
  • ACCENT_SYMBOL accent symbol
  • WARDROBE_STYLE wardrobe style
  • STYLE_FIDELITY_ANCHORS style fidelity anchors
  • SOURCE_CONTENT_TO_AVOID source content to avoid
  • ASPECT_RATIO aspect ratio
