What this style does

Part 4 of Everyday Riso: the cheerful bearded grandfather's afternoons — a giant harvest pumpkin, a winning xiangqi piece, an endless nap blanket and a huge fresh catch — in the same three-ink orange, olive and near-black risograph system on cream paper, with exactly one absurdly oversized object, visible misregistration and halftone grain, and one tiny black corner label.

Signature traits

  • Strict three-ink risograph on cream paper #F4EBDD: warm orange #FF6C2F and olive green #5E6B3A as broad masses, near-black #1E1E1E only for tiny eyes, thin round glasses, the label and slippers. Brown appears only where orange overprints olive.
  • The print must look physically printed: 1–3 px misregistration between ink layers, fine grain with patchy uneven coverage, coarse halftone or stipple dots for every shade, translucent overprint. No smooth digital gradient, airbrush, 3D or glow.
  • One recurring character: a cheerful elderly man with the series build — rounded blocky body, soft thick limbs, small bald head, big soft hands — a round paper-white beard and side hair left as bare cream paper (never white ink), small round black glasses, skin as pale orange halftone, and an olive cardigan, orange trousers and black slippers. Comfortable, never frail or sad.
  • Exactly one everyday object is absurdly oversized; the person and all other props stay at normal series proportions. The action is a plain daily routine and the mood is relaxed, warm and funny.
  • Exactly one tiny bold black sans-serif label of 1–3 uppercase words in a free top corner, about 1.7 percent of canvas height, in one or two lines; no headline and no other wording.
  • Pose and direction vary per case (sit, stand on tiptoe, kneel, squat, walk, ride) so a set of four never repeats one posture.

How to use

  1. Copy the style.json below.
  2. Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude, or any LLM image workflow (Nano Banana Pro, GPT Image, Midjourney).
  3. Swap the variables for your own subject and generate.
