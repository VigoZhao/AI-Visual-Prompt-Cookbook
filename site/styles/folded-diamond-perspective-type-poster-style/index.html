Folded Diamond Perspective Type Poster Style

Minimal diamond-aperture posters with folded perspective typography.

What this style does

A bold minimalist editorial poster style using low-angle hero photography inside a diamond aperture, folded tan paper or canvas planes, and oversized white perspective typography printed across the lower surface.

Signature traits

  • Preserve the core visual identity: A bold minimalist editorial poster style using low-angle hero photography inside a diamond aperture, folded tan paper or canvas planes, and oversized white perspective typography printed across the lower surface.
  • Shoot the subject as a real-photo low-angle hero image with wide-lens foreshortening, strong sunlight, and a bright cyan sky or clean outdoor backdrop.
  • Layout: place the photo inside a large rotated square diamond aperture in the upper half of a tactile folded tan paper or linen canvas surface.
  • Typography: set MAIN_TEXT in enormous white ultra-bold condensed sans-serif type across the lower half.
  • Make the type feel printed onto the folded surface, split by the central crease, distorted by perspective, cropped by the poster edges, and lightly textured by the paper grain.
  • Mood and finish: bold minimalism, streetwear editorial, monumental confidence, warm tan cardstock texture, crisp sunlight, cyan sky contrast, soft shadows, printed-paper grain, sharp geometric photo crop.

How to use

  1. Copy the style.json below.
  2. Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude, or any LLM image workflow (Nano Banana Pro, GPT Image, Midjourney).
  3. Swap the variables for your own subject and generate.

Variables

  • SUBJECT subject
  • SUBJECT_ACTION subject action
  • PRODUCT_OR_PROP product or prop
  • LOCATION location
  • BACKGROUND_ELEMENTS background elements
  • MAIN_TEXT main text
  • SECONDARY_TEXT secondary text
  • ACCENT_SYMBOL accent symbol
  • WARDROBE_STYLE wardrobe style
  • ASPECT_RATIO aspect ratio
