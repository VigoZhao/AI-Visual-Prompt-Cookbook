Neon Doodle Gallery Snapshot

Phone photos covered in hot neon diary doodles.

Open in gallery View on GitHub

What this style does

A candid phone-photo style layered with chaotic neon digital marker doodles: hot-pink and cyan subject outlines, yellow monster spikes, rough handwritten captions, stars, paw prints, spiderweb corners, scribble bars, halos, plants, and student diary energy.

Signature traits

  • Preserve the core visual identity: A candid phone-photo style layered with chaotic neon digital marker doodles: hot-pink and cyan subject outlines, yellow monster spikes, rough handwritten captions, stars, paw prints, spiderweb corners, scribble bars, halos, plants, and student diary energy.
  • Use BACKGROUND_ELEMENTS to control secondary scene details, textures, signage, props, and environmental cues.
  • Use a realistic candid phone-photo as the base layer.
  • The setting should feel specific and ordinary: visible walls, art, shelves, labels, tables, lamps, posters, people, bags, shadows, grain, and imperfect handheld framing.
  • Draw a loud digital marker layer directly on top of the photo.
  • Place rough uppercase handwritten marker text in open areas, using white, yellow, or lime green.

How to use

  1. Copy the style.json below.
  2. Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude, or any LLM image workflow (Nano Banana Pro, GPT Image, Midjourney).
  3. Swap the variables for your own subject and generate.

Variables

  • SUBJECT subject
  • SUBJECT_ACTION subject action
  • PRODUCT_OR_PROP product or prop
  • LOCATION location
  • BACKGROUND_ELEMENTS background elements
  • MAIN_TEXT main text
  • SECONDARY_TEXT secondary text
  • ACCENT_SYMBOL accent symbol
  • WARDROBE_STYLE wardrobe style
  • ASPECT_RATIO aspect ratio
