Portrait as Terrain

Monumental single-color landscapes made from human fragments, inhabited by tiny figures.

★ Star on GitHub Open in gallery View on GitHub

8 example cases

Same style.json, different subjects. Each case's values are in the examples section of the JSON.

What this style does

Monochrome human fragments become monumental single-color landscapes inhabited by tiny figures.

Signature traits

  • A high-contrast black-and-white photographic human fragment merges into one oversized tangible form that also reads as a landscape; both readings must be visible.
  • A tiny legible traveler or participant inhabits that landscape, creating a dramatic human-scale contradiction.
  • One saturated spot color dominates broad negative space and the transformed terrain, against deep black and paper-white portrait tones.
  • Spare editorial typography uses light or regular clean sans serif, with a large readable title and very small supporting line; typography leaves the visual metaphor clear.
  • Choose crop, direction and placement from the story; face visibility is optional and the carrier may be body, garment or hair.
  • Recompose portrait and landscape formats independently, keeping spacious fields and one dominant contour rather than squeezing one layout.

How to use

  1. Copy the style.json below.
  2. Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude, or any LLM image workflow (Nano Banana Pro, GPT Image, Midjourney).
  3. Swap the variables for your own subject and generate.

Variables

  • ASPECT_RATIO aspect ratio
  • STYLE_FIDELITY_ANCHORS style fidelity anchors
  • SOURCE_CONTENT_TO_AVOID source content to avoid
  • SUBJECT subject
  • METAPHOR metaphor
  • COLOR color
  • MAIN_TEXT main text
  • SECONDARY_TEXT secondary text
  • DESIGN_DIRECTION design direction
  • SUBJECT_ACTION subject action
  • PRODUCT_OR_PROP product or prop
  • LOCATION location
  • BACKGROUND_ELEMENTS background elements
  • ACCENT_SYMBOL accent symbol
  • WARDROBE_STYLE wardrobe style
